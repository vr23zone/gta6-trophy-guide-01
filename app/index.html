    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone: the story in chapter order, the stranger strands parked before the finale, then the free-roam sweep, the collections and the counters, and the 100% audit last. Hour figures are this guide's own estimates, sized from the official counts (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations, $250,000 to launder and the rest) — roughly 80 to 110 hours in all.

    Point of no return — the final mission. The official description of the finale promises nothing about the open world afterwards, so the roadmap treats it as one: finish all five stranger finales and Strangers & Freaks, and clear the missable-risk beats, before you start “You Gotta Know When to Get Off”. It is a precaution built from the descriptions, not a confirmed lock-out.

    Missable trophies

    Three trophies are flagged missable-risk, and the flags are built the only honest way available before anyone can check them in the game: from the official descriptions. One turns on choices, which are one-time by nature; one needs a failure followed by a success at what may be a one-time event; one points at a single partnership beat a story branch can pass by. Each flag says plainly that it is our reading of the description, not yet verified in-game. Everything else on the list is a free-roam activity, a repeatable robbery or a collection — earnable after the story — and the roadmap still parks the stranger strands before the finale as cheap insurance.

    The two habits that protect all three: keep a rotating manual save before big story missions from Chapter 3 on, and never let a dialogue choice lapse — answer all 500 of them. With those two habits, nothing on this page can be lost in a single playthrough.

    Save & settings

    Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) as you tick trophies and move counters — nothing leaves the machine, and the app works with the network off. Export a copy before you clear browser data or move devices, and import it on the other side.

    About this guide

    Every trophy's name, description and grade is the official PlayStation 5 list, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, the two agreeing on all 55; PSNProfiles also carries the progress targets PlayStation tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 500 choices and the rest), which are the targets of this tracker's counters. The guides, the roadmap, the hour estimates and the missable flags are this guide's own words and readings, written for this entry; where a description does not pin a fact down, the guide says so instead of inventing one. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere — the look is our own.
